## 🚀 Deployment Guide (How to Go Live)

To keep this project "Stealth," follow these steps to deploy it to a custom URL that won't look suspicious in your browser history.

### **Option 1: Vercel (Recommended for Stealth)**
Vercel uses randomized subdomains that are harder for schools to bulk-block.
1. Sign in to [Vercel](https://vercel.com) with your GitHub account.
2. Click **Add New** > **Project**.
3. Import this repository (`EDMATHS-KIT`).
4. **Pro Tip:** Change the "Project Name" to something boring like `math-homework-helper` or `physics-notes-v3`.
5. Click **Deploy**. Your games will be live on a `.vercel.app` link.

### **Option 2: Netlify**
1. Sign in to [Netlify](https://netlify.com) with GitHub.
2. Click **Add new site** > **Import an existing project**.
3. Select this repo and click **Deploy**.
4. Once deployed, go to **Site Settings** and change the site name to something like `assignment-tracker`.

### **Option 3: GitHub Pages**
1. Go to your repo **Settings** on GitHub.com.
2. Click **Pages** on the left sidebar.
3. Under "Build and deployment," set the source to **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Select **main** and click **Save**.
5. Your site will be live at `https://github.io`.
